# postman-recall

Resolve the installed `tmux-a2a-postman` executable and its `version`; do not
substitute a developer checkout or a downloaded procedure. Read and follow the
complete output of `tmux-a2a-postman help recall` for this request.
If that binary lacks the topic, report the incompatibility and stop this action.
