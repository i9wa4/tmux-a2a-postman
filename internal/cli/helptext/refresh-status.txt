Usage: tmux-a2a-postman refresh-status [--config <path>]

Collect one finite snapshot of the explicitly selected TMUX server, across all
sessions/windows/panes. TMUX must contain socket,PID,index; PID 0 means unknown.
No caller role or TMUX_PANE is required. --config is accepted for compatibility
but never read or used to choose membership. Configuration attribution comes
from provenance saved by verified normal operations in each workspace.

One nonblocking OS advisory lock protects refresh; busy returns skipped_busy
immediately with exit 0. Owner metadata is best effort. Long-held contention is
reported with PID/invocation/phase/age; never delete, steal or force-unlock it.
Refresh has a two-second total budget, a one-second capture sweep (64 panes,
200ms per native frame), and a 30-second cadence for each matching live process.
Partial coverage retains actual per-pane times; uncaptured panes remain due.
Unknown node summaries are counted separately from unavailable captures.

Atomic status.json is under the private state-root %status/socket-hash namespace.
Raw history remains there until manually deleted. No daemon, timer or pruning.
The prior snapshot survives prepublication failures. A post-rename sync failure
reports uncertain durability. Output: updated, skipped_busy, partial or error.
Partial/error exits nonzero and explains failure on stderr. Mail success and
notification safety do not depend on this supplemental observation.
