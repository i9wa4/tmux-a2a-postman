channel-sync [--config <absolute-postman.md>]

Bounded foreground intake and catch-up for the explicitly configured top-level
external filesystem binding. Requires its exact native TMUX socket/TMUX_PANE.
Reads inbox and read archives, recovers durable mail, atomically publishes reports
to outbox and returns pending exact decision filenames. Uses ordinary POP and the
shared generic tmux notification lock. No daemon, watcher or empty POP polling.

JSON success/as_of/channel/reason. accepted identifies durable mail; notification
is terminal input evidence only. published means local availability, not human
receipt. more=true requires another foreground scan after draining accepted mail.
Partial results remain durable on error; retry the same identities unchanged.
See docs/design/external-filesystem-channel.md for provisioning and recovery.
