send-heredoc — publish local workspace mail

Usage:
  tmux-a2a-postman send-heredoc --to <node> <<'POSTMAN_BODY'
  literal Markdown body
  POSTMAN_BODY

Options:
  --to <node>                    Configured adjacent recipient (required)
  --reply-required              Open an exact reply obligation
  --no-reply                    Do not open a reply obligation
  --reply-to <message-id>        Exact claimed conversation message
  --fills-input-request-id <id>  Exact required input to fill
  --capture-related-panes       Supplemental related-pane evidence
  --config <absolute-path>      Explicit postman.md override
  --postman-md <absolute-path>  Alias for --config

The body must come from nonterminal stdin and contain 1–61440 bytes. A
quoted delimiter keeps shell-sensitive Markdown literal. No body argv is
accepted. Sender identity comes from the native calling tmux pane and
configured role; the recipient must be a configured outgoing edge.

On success, separate private receiver-inbox and sender-sent copies are
durable. JSON status is sent and includes message, mandatory file-derived
self, and a separate notice outcome. The default route uses the registered
Bash/Readline hook (see help register-notifier). accepted means the key was
queued, not POP or reply. Missing/stale setup reports notice.status=unavailable;
transport ambiguity reports unknown. This never rolls back mail. Send does
not wait for recipient POP, reply, or task completion.
An uncertain send or notice is not permission for a blind retry.

The optional capture result cannot change the mail result. Native pane-text
capture currently reports unknown and no text until stable role attribution
is resolved.
